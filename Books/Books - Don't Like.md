Some books that I don't like.  They looked good enough to get but not to my taste.

### Hercule Poirot (Series)

On the plus side, I found a collection for [two dollars](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0CR1T96ZF) that was 1300 pages.  I made it about 2/3 of the way through before giving up.

- not the author's fault, but this edition was full of typos and missing pages
- perhaps it is bound to happen with so many stories, but I found them repetitive
- I also didn't always buy Poirot's solution as the only possible outcome
	- I couldn't always follow his logic -- it seemed like he had some hidden information, but perhaps this would be easier for an NT person to consume?
	- the logical leaps he makes are just the author's social intuition

### Red Mars

I picked this one up because people I know liked the series and Gemini recommended it with:

> It treats the **sociology and geology** of Mars as a massive, complex system. It’s like _The Expanse_ but with more focus on the "How-To" of building a world

Well, my first watch of The Expanse also fell flat, so maybe it takes a couple goes?

Anyway, I couldn't stand it -- the first character you follow is evil.  Or perhaps amoral.  Anyway, bad.  I couldn't get into it and I put it down.

### Neal Stephenson

The only one I am sure I read is *Snow Crash*.  It started off awesome, but then strange chakra stuff started happening and I wasn't sure (and no longer cared) what was going on.  I have heard good things about Criptonomicon and Anathem, but I am wary of reading anything more by this author.

### Dune (Series)

This seems like it would be right up my alley, but I only made it 30 pages in before never opening it again.  I found it boring and I didn't care.  Sure, some books take some time to get going, but given the hype, this was a real letdown.
