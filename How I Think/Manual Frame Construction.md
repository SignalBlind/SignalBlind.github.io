# Manual Frame Construction

Understanding the situation and determining:

- What are the specifications and requirements?
- What is my role? What is their goal? What are the rules here?
- This is a static computation -- figuring out the config file to apply

There is a potential for stalls here:

- If the situation is novel I have to decide what is going on
- If the situation is complex or ambiguous I may have to weigh different approaches
- If I am missing data, do I collect more data or proceed?
	- See [Functional Logic Modeling](Functional%20Logic%20Modeling.md) -- I have an optimization that lets me proceed on partial data

The output of this is something like a specification for what is required of the later steps and a label for what is going on.

It is hard to introspect and see the exact mechanism here so this feels a bit hand-wavy to me.  Regardless there is _a_ step where I decide what I am trying to do and this is it.
