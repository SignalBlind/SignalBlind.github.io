## About Signal Blindness

Signal blindness is a term used to describe the experience of individuals who lack social salience.

### Personal Experience

This site documents my personal journey and experiences as someone who is signal blind. Through sharing these experiences, I hope to:

1. Raise awareness about signal blindness
2. Help others who may have similar experiences
3. Foster understanding and empathy
4. Build a community of support

### Contact

If you have questions or want to share your own experiences, feel free to reach out through GitHub.
